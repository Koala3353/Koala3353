rules we are held to onsite

COA · equipment

Permitted and prohibited

Permitted

  • Decorations aligned with the RecWeek theme
  • Laptops, cameras, fans, on one socket
  • Masking tape, and only masking tape
  • Designs hung on the tent frame, if they do not damage it

Prohibited

  • Extra tables and chairs, panel boards
  • Oversized standees (1m by 0.75m)
  • Extension cords, speakers, cooking appliances
  • Thumbtacks, double-sided tape, duct tape, scotch tape, packing tape, sticky tack
  • Anything stuck to the tent canopy

Promotional materials

  • Nothing posted on walls, floors, or pillars anywhere on campus (L7, major)
  • Each org answers for where its materials end up
  • Clear exceptions with COA Logistics beforehand
Only equipment approved in the Logistics Declaration Form may be used. Undeclared equipment is a major violation (L11).
COA · etiquette

At the booth

Area of responsibility

  • Your booth space, the shared walkways, and 1.5m in front
  • Clean and orderly at all times
  • An unclean AoR after egress is a minor violation (L6)
  • One box under the table is allowed, nothing else left behind

Applications

  • Applying happens online through the standardised form
  • COA provides the QR code for the booth
  • Do not run registrations inside the booth, it causes congestion
  • Payment method and proof go on the form itself

Damage

  • Vandalism or damaging adhesives: major violation (L9)
  • Destroying provided equipment: severe violation (SV7)
  • Return all COA materials complete and on time (L14)
COA · contingencies

Contingency plans

Rain

  • Unplug booth equipment immediately
  • Return items to the org room in MVP
  • Keep plastic sheets or waterproof tarps on hand
  • If it persists, booths relocate to MVP Roofdeck
  • COA-M is not liable for props left behind

Class suspension

  • Gimmicks pause, then get rescheduled to a later slot
  • OLEs may continue online, move, or be cancelled
  • Application forms stay open online
  • Keep an online fallback ready

Org Parade

  • Delayed start in bad weather
  • Contingency route if heavy rain continues
  • Otherwise rescheduled within the week
  • Route changes announced by COA

Shiftings · rules

Org rep guidelines

Headcount

  • Three official representatives at any time
  • Two inside the booth
  • One rover, photographer, caller, or marketer
  • Photographers count as reps (L3)
  • All three wear Org IDs at all times (S2)

Coverage

  • At least one rep at the booth from opening to closing
  • Unmanned for 30 minutes is a violation (L2)
  • Reaching quota does not allow closing the booth, post a sign instead
  • Avoid prolonged crowding at the booth

Rover limits

  • Red Brick Road
  • Zen Garden and Colayco Pavilion
  • Promenade
  • Anywhere else is a major violation (L8)
  • Each day needs 2 shifts with a DocPub, DigiC, or Prod Design member on them
Shiftings · turnover

Handing over a shift

  • The incoming shift arrives before the outgoing shift leaves. No gaps.
  • Both groups take one photo together, everyone from the previous shift and everyone from the next.
  • Send that photo to the Core Team GC. That is the turnover record.
  • Hand over the Org IDs. Three IDs, three people.
If you cannot make your shift, find your own replacement and inform the PMs. An unmanned booth costs the org ₱150 to ₱600.
Shiftings · sign up

The shifting sheet

  • Four days, seven blocks a day, three slots a block.
  • Fill in your availability tonight. Heads answer for their committee’s coverage.
  • The PMs do a final pass and fill the gaps. Empty slots get assigned.

Mahjong Leaderboard

testing before we go live

Leaderboard

How it works

Getting in

  • Scan the table QR (?table=A1)
  • Register with name and student ID
  • One player starts a session
  • Everyone else joins with the PIN

Playing

  • All four seats fill before a game can be submitted
  • All four players confirm a result before it records
  • Phases: CASUAL, TAMBAY, TOURNAMENT
  • Projector mode: ?display=1

Why it matters

  • One leaderboard accumulating across the Mixer and Welcome Week
  • Feeds RecWeek Wrapped and the data report
  • DigiC needs admin access before the Mixer
Leaderboard

Testing round

  • Register, start a session, join with a PIN, submit a game, then confirm it.
  • Try the edge cases: submit with three players, refuse to confirm, refresh mid-game, register twice.
  • Report anything confusing or broken in the RECSTRAT synergy chat: what you did, what you expected, what happened.
